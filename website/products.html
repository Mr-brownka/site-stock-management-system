Questions

Good to know

Do we have to stop using Zoho Books?

No. Ostock reads your items and goods received from Zoho Books. It never changes anything in Zoho.

What phones does it work on?

Any modern Android phone or iPhone, through the browser. You can add it to the home screen like an app. It is light on data.

What if the site has no network?

Keep working. Entries are saved on the phone and sent when the signal comes back.

How long does it take to start a new site?

About a day. Connect Zoho, upload the first count from Excel, and the storekeeper starts that evening.

Who can see what?

Storekeepers enter their own store. Managers and procurement can be given view-only access to the sites they need.
